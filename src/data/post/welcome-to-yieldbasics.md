YieldBasics is a new site about **yields**: the returns you can earn by holding, lending or staking an asset — in crypto, stocks, bonds and beyond.

## What is coming

- **Guides** that explain where a yield comes from and what risks come with it.
- **Tools and calculators** to compare yields and see what a rate means over time.
